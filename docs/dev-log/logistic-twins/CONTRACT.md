## Symbolic alignment
Y_i ~ Bernoulli(p_i), logit(p_i)=alpha+beta*x_i. Numeric finite x and numeric y exactly 0/1; equal lengths n>2, unit weights, intercept plus one predictor. DGP: independently standard-normal x; Bernoulli y at declared probabilities. Fitters receive data without truth. Extractor maps intercept to alpha and slope to beta. Recovery compares these named log-odds coefficients. Logistic intervals are normal Wald at 0.90 and 0.95; Gaussian intervals stay Student t.

## Exact finite-MLE diagnosis
Reject all-zero/all-one outcomes and constant predictor with explicit messages. For response-class supports [a0,b0],[a1,b1], complete separation is b0<a1 or b1<a0; quasi separation is equality in either direction with predictor variation. Unique finite MLE requires b0>a1 and b1>a0. Exact comparisons, no support tolerance/jitter. Include singleton/tied finite-MLE case. Rounded fitted probabilities alone do not diagnose separation.

## Julia numerical repair
Keep logistic_mle signature, level=.95, maxiter=80, tol=1e-9 and FitResult schema. Validate controls. Use c=xmin/2+xmax/2, s=max(abs(x-c)), z=(x-c)/s; fit gamma0+gamma1*z. beta=gamma1/s; alpha=gamma0-c*beta. Finite large slopes are allowed; transformed nonfinite points fail explicitly.
Stable loss is softplus((1-2*y)*eta); stable residual is sigmoid(-eta) for y=1 and -sigmoid(eta) for y=0. Stable weights exp(-abs(eta))/(1+exp(-abs(eta)))^2. Mean score g=Z'*(y-p)/n, mean information J=Z'*W*Z/n. Use PD factorization solve Delta=J\g. Start at zero. Armijo L(gamma+lambda*Delta)<=L(gamma)-1e-4*lambda*g'Delta; lambda starts 1, at most 40 halvings. Floating comparison allowance only 8eps(Float64)*max(1,abs(L)).
Convergence after recomputation requires max(abs(g))<=tol, max(abs(Z*Delta))<=tol, g'Delta/2<=tol^2/2. A damped small step or small objective change alone is insufficient. maxiter=0 is an iteration-limit failure for finite-MLE data. Document new standardized interpretation of tol. Recompute final information; solve covariance and transform full matrix. Point failures reject all points; interval-only failures keep points and omit affected intervals, with explicit message. No FitResult diagnostics extension.
